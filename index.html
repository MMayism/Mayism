THE ARTIST BEHIND THE ART

Hi, I'm Pheemwara.

A Thai illustrator with a background in architecture.

I have a degree in Architecture and professional experience in drafting and environmental impact assessment documentation in Thailand. Since 2023, I've been working as a freelance illustrator, creating novel cover illustrations for authors.

My architecture background has given me experience in design, composition and visual detail. Through MAYISM, I bring that foundation into character-focused art and storytelling.
