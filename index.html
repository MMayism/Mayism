OH, HI THERE! ♡

I'm Pheemwara,
but you can call me MAYISM.

I’m an illustrator who loves creating characters, emotional scenes and visual stories. I enjoy exploring the little details — expressions, lighting and atmosphere — that help an image feel alive.

My work moves between digital illustration and traditional drawing, with a special love for novel covers and character-focused art.
