/ 03 — About me · ศิลปินไทย

Hi, I’m
Pheemwara.

I draw people, stories and feelings — with the small details that make an image feel alive.

I’m MAYISM — my artist name — creating novel covers, character illustrations and visual studies that begin with feeling. I move between digital painting and traditional drawing, following whatever the image needs — a softer edge, a stronger shadow, a quieter expression.
